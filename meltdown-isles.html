How a match goes

Each side builds a base, then crews take turns: walk, dig, build and cast one spell. You get five seconds to retreat after your shot. Anything cut off from the ground falls. From turn 30 the lava rises every turn.

Materials

  • Earth, grass Soft. Blasts dig into it, lava slowly melts it.
  • Stone Shrugs off the edge of a blast. Lava cannot melt it.
  • Metal Only the core of a blast breaks it.
  • Sand Falls and piles up. Melts in lava.
  • Water Puts out fire and burning alchemists. Lava plus water makes stone.
  • Oil Soaks alchemists and burns hot. Barrels are full of it.
  • Acid Eats almost anything, alchemists included. Water neutralises it.
  • Ice Frost bolts freeze water. Fire melts it back.
  • Swamp gas Hides in caves. Any spark turns the whole cloud into a fireball.
  • Coal Smoulders for ages once lit, eating the ground away from below.
  • Slime Bounces alchemists and spells. No fall damage.
  • Fizzrock Explodes when water touches it. Chains are common.
  • Gunpowder Explodes in fire. Paint it for traps.

Hover over anything in the world to see what it is.

Walk with A / D. W jumps (hold it in the air for jet boots), S backflips. Point to aim, hold the mouse or Space to charge, release to fire. E or Tab opens the spell grid, and 1–9 pick spells directly. Mouse wheel zooms, right-drag pans, V snaps back.
