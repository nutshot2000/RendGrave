RENDGRAVE - Early Access (Act I)
================================

A pixel-simulated action RPG. Every enemy is built from layered pixels - armour over
cloth over flesh over bone - so blades take limbs off, acid melts them, fire chars them
and frost shatters them. The world burns, floods and explodes too.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Unzip the folder anywhere and run Rendgrave.exe. Keep the "assets" folder next to it
(it holds the music).

Needs Windows 10 or 11 and a graphics card that supports DirectX 12 or Vulkan.

CONTROLS
--------
WASD                Move
Left mouse          Attack (aim at the legs to take them off)
Right mouse         Cast the selected spell
1-9 / mouse wheel   Choose a spell
Space               Dodge roll
F                   Drink a potion
Q                   Throw an oil flask (if you chose flasks from Elder Maren)
E                   Interact: exits, stairs, waypoints, chests, shrines, the merchant
I                   Gear: click to equip, right click to sell (rare items: right click twice)
K or Tab            Skill tree (A / D or click to change school)
Esc                 Pause menu (controls, sound, screen shake, fullscreen, quit)
M / N               Music / sound on and off
- / +               Volume
F11                 Fullscreen
R                   After dying: wake in Ashford

GAMEPAD (Xbox names; PlayStation and others work the same way)
Just pick it up and press a button - the game switches over, and back again
the moment you touch the mouse or keyboard.
Left stick          Move
Right stick         Aim. Let go and you auto-aim at the nearest enemy in front of you.
                    Push it gently toward legs or the Hollow King's knees to aim low.
A                   Attack - or interact, when something is in reach
X / Y / B           Cast spell 1 / 2 / 3
LT                  Cast the selected spell
D-pad left / right  Choose a spell
RB                  Dodge roll
LB                  Drink a potion
RT                  Throw an oil flask
D-pad up            Skill tree
D-pad down / Back   Gear
Start               Pause menu
In menus            The left stick (or d-pad) moves a pointer: A clicks, X right-clicks, B goes back

YOUR SAVE
---------
The game saves by itself when you change area, open chests, change gear and quit.
The save is rendgrave_save.txt next to Rendgrave.exe, with the previous save kept as
rendgrave_save.txt.bak. If a save is ever damaged, the game loads the backup instead.
Settings are in rendgrave_settings.txt and rendgrave_options.txt.

Dungeons are rebuilt every time you enter them. If you quit inside one, you continue
at its entrance. When a dungeon's boss dies, a portal opens to take you to any waypoint.
Elder Maren gives you a gift for the road after your first talk, and later she can
reset your skills for a little gold (talk to her again). If you die you wake in Ashford and lose a tenth of your gold. Your gear is safe.

WHERE TO GO
-----------
Talk to Elder Maren by the fire in Ashford. The line under the area name always says what
to do next, and a gold marker (or an arrow at the edge of the screen) points the way.
Roads are lined with lanterns, and every exit is marked with lit posts and its name.
The minimap fills in as you explore.

WHAT'S IN ACT I
---------------
Ashford, the Hollow Pines, the Sunken Crypt, the Old Graveyard, the Blackwater Fen,
Ravenhold and its cellars. Two bosses: the Hollow King and the Bandit Chief.
54 skills in six schools, loot with affixes and legendaries, powder kegs, oil slicks,
swamp gas, and a merchant in town.

Act II - The Frozen North - is coming in an update.

Thank you for playing!
